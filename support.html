Carioca Chile — Support

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Thanks for playing Carioca Chile. This page answers the most common questions. If you don't find what you need, write to dev@peltriaux.com.

How do I play?

Carioca is a nine-round rummy game from Chile. Each round has its own objective, from two trios up to the Escala Real. On your turn you draw a card, lay your objective once you have it, extend melds already on the table, and end by discarding. You can't lay on your very first turn. The round ends when a player goes out, and the lowest total after all the rounds wins.

The tutorial opens on your first deal. Reopen it any time from the ? button in a game or from Options. A quick-rules sheet is in the same menu, and the full rules are on the web at peltriaux.com/cariocachile/rules.html.

How does online play work?

From the home screen, tap Online Multiplayer, then Play online. You join a live queue, and the game starts by itself a few seconds after a second player arrives. Up to four people can join the same table.

If nobody comes after a minute, you can play against the computer instead, or ask to be notified. Alert me when a table opens does the same from the start: pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes, go do something else, and you get a notification when a game is waiting for you.

How do I play with friends?

In Online Multiplayer, tap Host a new game. You get a room code such as brave-otter-4729 and a link to share. Your friends tap the link, or choose Join with a code and type it. Up to four people can play, and you can fill empty seats with computer players.

If someone drops out, a computer player takes their seat after a few minutes. If the app closed, open it again and tap Rejoin game on the home screen. If the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.

To play in the same room without internet, open the Same room tab. Host Nearby Game and Join Nearby Game connect phones over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Pass & Play lets 2 to 4 people share one device.

What are Duels?

A duel is a challenge on one exact deal. Play it, then send the link to a friend. They play the same cards once, and you both see who scored lower. Duels are free and expire after seven days.

What does the unlock include?

Your first three solo games are free. A one-time purchase of $2.99 adds unlimited solo games, Quick Play, the Daily and Weekly Challenges, Pass & Play, and hosting private games for friends.

Always free, with or without the unlock: the online queue, joining a friend's game by link or code, nearby games, and duels. Gems are earned by playing and are never sold.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Options on the home screen (or the ⋯ menu in a game) and tap Restore purchases. Apple checks your Apple ID and unlocks the app if it finds the purchase. If it doesn't and you're sure you bought it, email dev@peltriaux.com with the date and the Apple ID you used.

Why didn't my score appear on a leaderboard?

Best Score and Weekly Best Score only count games played with the standard nine rounds and without Double Down, so every score on them is comparable. Wins still count with House Rules.

Games where you asked the Coach for a hint, Quick Play games, Pass & Play games and nearby games don't post to Game Center. Scores from a test or sideloaded build go to Apple's sandbox, not the real boards.

How do notifications work?

Carioca can tell you when someone is waiting for an online game, and remind you about your daily streak, the weekly challenge and season rewards about to expire. Turn these off in Options → Multiplayer hints.

The your game is ready notification is the one you ask for with Alert me when a table opens, so it is sent even when hints are off. To stop all notifications, turn them off for Carioca in iOS Settings.

How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed into iCloud and iCloud sync is on in Options (it is by default), your settings, stats, gems and in-progress solo game travel between your iPhone and iPad. Online games don't sync through iCloud; rejoin them from the home screen.

How do I change the language?

Open Options → Language. Carioca speaks English, Spanish, French and Portuguese. System follows your device's language.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes, from version 3.3. Unfolded, you get the full iPad table. Half-folded on a table, your opponents and the deck sit on the upright half, and your hand and the melds sit on the flat half. Closed, it plays like any iPhone.

Accessibility

Carioca follows iOS Reduce Motion and larger text sizes, and its buttons are labelled for VoiceOver. In Options you can also turn off haptics, sound, lobby music and the deal animation, or switch on fast animations.

I found a bug or have a suggestion
